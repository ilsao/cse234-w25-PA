`myAllreduce` is slower because it performs a linear reduction in which rank 0 receives and reduces (N-1) full arrays sequentially, making rank 0 a communication bottleneck. 
MPI's optimized `Allreduce` may use tree-based, recursive-doubling, or ring algorithms depending on the message size and process count, allowing communication to proceed more concurrently. 
Therefore, the time difference comes mainly from serialization and implementation optimizations.
